# Landing-page assets

- `university-navarra.svg`: unmodified official University of Navarra logo from
  https://www.unav.edu/o/unav-principal-theme/images/unav-black-logo.svg .
- `cima.jpg`: CIMA logo supplied by the project owner, displayed without alteration
  on a white background and linked to https://cima.cun.es/ .
- `tecnun.jpg`: Tecnun logo supplied by the project owner, displayed without
  alteration on a white background and linked to https://tecnun.unav.edu/ .
- `p1crc-tissue.png`: the existing Human CRC example's low-resolution tissue image,
  copied without modification from
  `data/CRC_P1/VisiumHD/binned_outputs/square_002um/spatial/tissue_lowres_image.png`.
  The source-data paper is Oliveira et al. (2025),
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02193-3 . The landing page links the biological
  walkthrough and credits this example separately from the software citation.

The SVG overlay in `templates/tissue_preview.html` is an illustrative spatial
grid/ROI demonstration, labelled as such; it is not an expression or segmentation
result. Institution marks remain the property of their respective institutions.

Author affiliation and public contact were verified against
https://en.unav.edu/web/biomedical-engineering-centre/areas-of-research/biologia-computacional-e-ia .
Project links follow the repository's configured Git origin.
