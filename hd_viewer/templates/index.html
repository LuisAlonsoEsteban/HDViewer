A connected workflow

Start with your tissue.
Follow your question.

Use your own spatial data or explore an example. Each layer stays connected to the tissue, so your comparisons keep their spatial context.

  1. 01

    Load & prepare

    Choose an expression matrix, tissue positions, and an image. Add a single-cell reference and select optional preprocessing.

  2. 02

    Explore & define

    Find genes and cell populations. Use linked views, native barcodes, and regions of interest to define your comparison.

  3. 03

    Compare & interpret

    Examine markers, differential expression, and population composition. Follow the worked colorectal cancer example for a complete analysis.
